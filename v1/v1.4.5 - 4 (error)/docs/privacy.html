Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026 · Version v1.4.5 · Contact: linkivo.web@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Linkivo collects only the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account data: your name, email address, and profile photo, obtained through Firebase Authentication (Google or Email/Password sign-in).
  • User content: links, folder names, descriptions, tags, and history that you save within the app — stored in Firebase Realtime Database under your unique user ID.
  • Settings: your theme, sort preferences, app lock PIN, and other configuration — stored in Firebase under your user ID.
  • Profile photos: images you upload are hosted on imgbb via a secure Cloudflare Worker proxy.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Linkivo service.
  • To sync your data across devices via Firebase Realtime Database.
  • To restore deleted items from the Recycle Bin (30-day retention).
  • We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal data.

3. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication), governed by Firebase's Privacy Policy. Firebase Security Rules ensure that each user can only access their own data. Your app lock PIN is stored encrypted at rest in Firebase.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and database.
  • imgbb: image hosting for profile photos and folder icons, accessed via our Cloudflare Worker proxy.
  • allorigins.win: optional CORS proxy used to fetch link thumbnails (og:image). No personal data is transmitted.
  • Google Favicons API: used to display site favicons. No personal data is transmitted.

5. Your Rights

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time via Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from Firebase. For questions or data requests, contact linkivo.web@gmail.com.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Linkivo uses browser localStorage to cache preferences (theme, sort, sidebar state) for faster loading. No cross-site tracking cookies are used.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at linkivo.web.app/privacy. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
