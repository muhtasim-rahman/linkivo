Step 7 of 13

Link preview

Open a link inside Linkivo with tabs for page, video, image and details.

1Tabs

  • Page: the site itself when it allows being shown; otherwise Linkivo skips to a tab that works.
  • Video and Image: playable or full-size when the site offers them.
  • Details: title, description, address, and which service supplied the data.

2Good to know

Some sites refuse to be shown inside another page. That is the site's choice; use Open in a new tab.
